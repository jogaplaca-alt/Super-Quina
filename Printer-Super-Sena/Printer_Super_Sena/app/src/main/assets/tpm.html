How to find the result yourself

  • Print width is less than paper width.
  • The image of the test line is calculated for a density of 8 dots per millimeter.
  • The correct width must be a multiple of 8.
  • Printers are divided into two types. The first type does not print dots after the maximum number. The second type prints them on a new line.
